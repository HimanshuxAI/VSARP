## 🎨 GLOBAL DESIGN SYSTEM
**Apply this theme to the entire master slide template.**

### 1. Color Palette (Theme: "Future Tech Trust")
*   **Background:** `#0F172A` (Deep Slate Blue) - *Dark Mode is professional & easy on eyes.*
*   **Primary Accent:** `#3B82F6` (Electric Blue) - *For Headings, important icons.*
*   **Secondary Accent:** `#10B981` (Emerald Green) - *For "Success", "Verified", "Growth" metrics.*
*   **Text Color:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) - *High contrast white-ish text.*
*   **Muted Text:** `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) - *For subtitles and less important details.*

### 2. Typography (Fonts)
*   **Headings (Titles):** `Montserrat` or `Poppins` (Bold / Extra Bold).
    *   *Why?* Strong, geometric, modern.
*   **Body Text:** `Inter` or `Open Sans` (Regular).
    *   *Why?* Extremely readable for bullet points.
*   **Code / Technical Terms:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Consolas`.
    *   *Why?* Shows this is a technical project.

### 3. Visual Style
*   **Shapes:** Rounded corners (Border-radius: 12px) on pictures/boxes.
*   **Shadows:** Soft glow effects (e.g., Blue glow behind key screenshots).
*   **Icons:** Use "Lucide" or "Feather" style line icons (Clean, thin strokes, White color).

## SLIDE 3: Problem Definition
